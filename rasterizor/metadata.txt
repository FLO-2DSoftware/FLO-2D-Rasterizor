about=FLO-2D Rasterizor converts four-column FLO-2D grid output records (grid element ID, X coordinate, Y coordinate, and output value) into georeferenced GeoTIFF rasters. It supports mapping results such as depth, velocity, water surface elevation, and time-related outputs. The plugin also compares two raster layers and styles the difference raster.
